How a weekly read works

1 · digest

Turn the files into numbers

siteread counts people by where they first came from. It adds your Search Console weeks. It verifies every crawler against its vendor's list.

npx siteread digest --gsc export.zip \
  --log access.log --out .siteread/digests
2 · memo

Read it as a dated memo

The seo-read skill for Claude Code writes up the week. It covers what changed, where people came from and which crawlers read what. It ends with two to four next steps.

{{ install }}
3 · share (optional)

See where you stand

Send one week of numbers to the benchmark. You see where each number ranks. The CLI prints every byte first. Nothing is sent without your yes.

npx siteread share --dry-run

Read the docs for every command, the share payload and the library.
